# Series plan
The first three titles now have complete manuscript drafts. Later titles remain working proposals. Series title The Wonder Crew is unconfirmed and not cleared.

## Formats
Character-led adventures: one child's emotional experience leads; friends can appear.
Collective adventures: a shared challenge needs multiple perspectives.
Participatory activity books: children add inventions, drawings and decisions.

## First collection and later order
1. book:map — The Map Beneath the Sunflowers: collective introduction.
2. book:zuri — Zuri and the Footprints After Rain: examine clues and change one's mind.
3. book:kofi — Kofi and the Almost-Perfect Machine: share imperfect work.
4. book:crew2 — Second collective adventure: story not yet selected.

## Later character titles
book:amara — Amara and the Unfinished Wall: shared creativity.
book:eli — Eli and the Game Nobody Knew: being a beginner.
book:nia — Nia and the Very Loud Saturday: asking for what one needs.
book:tayo — Tayo and the Night of No Stars: adapting when plans change.

## Possible later settings
London museum; British seaside; countryside stay; Welsh family holiday; family visits overseas. Specific destinations and family connections are not established. Local characters should have their own agency and knowledge. Research locations and cultural details when developing those books.

Each book must be satisfying independently. Recurring objects enrich the experience without making another purchase necessary to understand an ending.
