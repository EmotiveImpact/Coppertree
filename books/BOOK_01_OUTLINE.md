# Book 1: The Map Beneath the Sunflowers
ID book:map | Status: outline proposal, not manuscript
Primary emotional question: Can I share something that feels like mine?

## Proposed story sequence
1. During an adult-supported garden clean-up, Zuri notices a tin beneath a sunflower bed.
2. It contains an unfinished neighbourhood map, an old photograph and a message: For whoever finds this: there is still one thing we never finished.
3. The friends interpret the clues differently. Tayo suspects a spaceship.
4. Zuri treats the discovery as hers, rushes ahead and dismisses an idea. This causes a setback; its exact mechanism is not yet written.
5. Sharing observations helps the crew understand the clues and learn about their neighbourhood.
6. They find a forgotten gathering corner and help revive it with the community.
7. With adult help and permission, the shed becomes The Lookout.
8. A neighbourhood celebration includes space for the reader to draw themselves.
9. The tin becomes their first discovery-shelf exhibit. They leave a new message inside.

## Threads to resolve before drafting
Who made the map, when, why the task was unfinished, and why nobody has completed it since. Exact clue count and route. Relationship between gathering corner and shed. Zuri's mistake, apology and repair must be concrete and proportionate. Avoid presenting abandoned or unsafe places as places children should enter alone.

## Participation possibilities
Draw yourself joining the crew; compare clues; complete a mural; design a helpful invention; suggest a way to include a friend. These are creative activities, not diagnostic assessments.
