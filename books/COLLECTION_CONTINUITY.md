# First collection continuity

Version 0.1, 9 November 2026. Manuscript continuity for the first three books.

## Reading and event order

1. The Map Beneath the Sunflowers establishes the Lookout. The shed is an ordinary garden shed at the beginning. The community restores the space after permission and adult checks. The opening follows a week of preparation.
2. Zuri and the Footprints After Rain takes place after the Lookout exists. Mrs Bennett's garden wildlife camera records a fox making the trail. Zuri replaces her first guess with evidence. A wildlife notebook joins the discovery shelf.
3. Kofi and the Almost-Perfect Machine also takes place after the Lookout exists. Uncle Deji supports a manual cardboard pencil sorter. The crew tests and improves it. Kofi's original drawing joins the discovery shelf.

Books 2 and 3 are presented in this reading order. Their precise spacing in story time remains open. Each book introduces enough context to stand alone.
