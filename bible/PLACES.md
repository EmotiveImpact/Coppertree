# Places

All names and geography are provisional.

## Copper Tree [place:neighbourhood]
Fictional London neighbourhood.

## Copper Tree Garden [place:garden]
Copper beech, vegetable beds, pond and an old shed.

## The Lookout [place:lookout]
Proposed clubhouse made from the garden shed during book 1; six mismatched seats, discovery shelf, biscuit tin and growing map.

## The Arches [place:arches]
Small businesses beneath the railway, including the repair workshop.

## Saturday Market [place:market]
Food, flowers, fabrics and second-hand finds.

## Copper Tree Library [place:library]
Books, rainy-day refuge and local-history cupboard.

## The Rec [place:rec]
Football, scooters and puddles.

The Lookout must not be treated as an established clubhouse before its creation in book 1. See the [proposed neighbourhood map and route notes](../assets/visual-reference/v0.1/README.md#neighbourhood-map). Exact distances and addresses remain open.
