# Copper Tree colouring artwork standard

Version 1, 9 November 2026. Based on the user's approval of the cleaned standalone Zuri treatment. This is a production guide, not an installed agent skill.

## Reference hierarchy
1. Original full-colour cast defines faces, proportions, clothes, hairstyles and identity. Its THEO label is historical; use Tayo in new text.
2. Approved cleaned Zuri defines black-and-white rendering: clear moderate outlines, simplified interior marks, open white areas.
3. Manuscripts and collection continuity define scene events and props.
Do not use the superseded 22-page book art as the character/style authority.

## Rendering
- Pure white colourable interiors; clean black contour lines.
- No grey fills, shading, gradients, hatching, stippling, sketch residue or decorative scratch marks.
- Clear outer contours with slightly lighter meaningful internal lines. Do not increase outline weight simply to conceal weak anatomy.
- Preserve expressive facial features. Black pupils and simple eyebrows are acceptable; avoid large filled areas elsewhere.
- Braids, beads, puffs, curls, twists and Bantu knots must retain each child's identity. Use open simplified shapes rather than dense strand texture.
- Clothing keeps its characteristic cut, pockets and major folds; remove redundant creases, dense stitching, tiny tread and lace clutter.
- Glass stays white inside its outline. Leaves need only a few veins. No ground shadows.

## Composition
Complete, enjoyable scenes with a clear focal action and meaningful surrounding objects. Generous colouring spaces, readable silhouettes and safe print margins. Vary composition and cast size across scenes. No repeated pictures to reach the page count. Backgrounds should enrich the adventure without overwhelming the characters.

## Generation brief template
Supply the actual original cast reference and approved cleaned style reference. State book/page ID, exact characters, action, location, story-time state, required props, composition and omissions. Request no generated text; captions and puzzle instructions are typeset separately. Retain generation history and review status.

## Review every image
Check identity against original cast, approved line treatment, anatomy, hands, outfit continuity, prop mechanics, scene chronology, open hair shapes, absence of grey and stray marks, page margins, duplicate objects and effective resolution. Inspect at intended print size. Request an edit or regenerate failures; never silently relax the standard.

## Scope and limits
Approval of the Zuri rendering authorises applying the treatment across the collection. It does not approve every subsequent image automatically. Full scene review and print checks remain required. Keep original source images, revised images and rejected drafts distinguishable. A reusable skill may be created after this workflow is demonstrated successfully.
