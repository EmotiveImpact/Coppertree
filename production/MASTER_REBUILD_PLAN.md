# Copper Tree master rebuild plan
Updated 9 November 2026. Active brief supersedes the 22-page editions as delivery targets.

## Confirmed scope
Three substantial colouring-led books, each with 50 content pages plus an additional cover. Preserve the original full-colour character identities. The user rejects the heavier, simplified character treatment in the existing interiors. Preserve those earlier files as superseded drafts.
Primary production destination: Amazon KDP paperback. Canva preparation is explicitly not required. Website and other print-on-demand products are possible later phases, not current deliverables.

## Content allocation per book
30 full-page colouring scenes; eight puzzles; six illustrated affirmations; four guided writing pages; one welcome page; one answer page. Blank reverse pages do not count towards these 50 content pages. If answers require additional readable space, expand the edition rather than shrink text or remove colouring pages. Covers are additional.
Working audience: ages 5 to 8. Short captions, joyful Black childhood, recognisable characters, complete pictures with generous colouring areas. Avoid drawing prompts as substitutes for finished artwork.

## Individual intentions
1. The Map Beneath the Sunflowers: belonging, sharing discoveries and community. Garden, market, library and Lookout scenes; routes, hidden objects, matching and observation puzzles. Affirmations about belonging, ideas and collaboration.
2. Zuri and the Footprints After Rain: curiosity, observation and changing your mind. Wildlife, rain, footprints and garden exploration; evidence matching, mazes and visual sequences. Affirmations about questions, learning and asking for help.
3. Kofi and the Almost-Perfect Machine: experimentation, resilience and accepting help. Workshop, pencil sorter, test runs and collaborative inventions; sorting, patterns and logical routes. Affirmations about practice, mistakes and teamwork.
Existing manuscripts and collection continuity remain source material; concise image-led storytelling replaces lengthy story/activity spreads.

## KDP production specification (proposed pending layout proof)
Use 8.5 x 11 inch portrait paperback, black ink on white paper, full-colour cover, no-bleed interior with all artwork inside safe margins. This is a proposed production choice, not a user-confirmed trim.
50 content pages are distinct from physical PDF page count. Proposed single-sided content with blank reverses yields 100 interior pages per book. Confirm final pagination before computing cover spine.
Separate interior PDF and wraparound cover PDF containing back, spine and front. Generate the cover using KDP's template for the actual trim, paper and final page count; do not reuse the A4 home-print cover.
Source images must support effective 300 ppi at placed size; do not claim enlarging low-resolution art creates detail. Embed fonts, check margins/gutter against current KDP requirements, and keep cover and interior exports separate.
Run KDP Print Previewer and order a physical proof before calling the books publication-ready. Account upload, pricing, publication and physical proof are not completed.
Record AI-generated artwork accurately during KDP submission.
